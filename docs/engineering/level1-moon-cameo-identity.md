# Level 1 Moon Cameo Identity Contract

Moon Cameo is now rendered by the same Match-3-owned `MoonCameoRitualView` component across:

- Level 1 prestart reward promise,
- terminal win/reward result,
- Curio Collection / Reliquary,
- awakened Curio Boutique cabinet.

The House no longer owns a separate duplicate cameo drawing implementation. The shared component preserves smoked moonstone, antique-gold nested rings, six moon-sigil echoes and the rose catalogue drop, while each surface controls only framing/context through its mode.

This keeps the Level 1 reward visually continuous from promise → acquisition → persistent world consequence without external or paid art dependencies.
