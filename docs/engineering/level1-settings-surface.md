# Level 1 Settings Surface

House Settings is now a real Level 1 product surface rather than a raw checkbox list.

- Audio controls are grouped under Audio and route through Master/Music/SFX buses.
- Haptics, Reduced Motion and High Contrast are grouped under Experience.
- Settings remain device-local and persistent through Match3UserSettings.
- The complete journey visual artifact now includes a rendered Settings screen.

No paid UI kit, plugin or asset is used.
