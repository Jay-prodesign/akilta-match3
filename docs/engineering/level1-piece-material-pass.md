# Level 1 Piece Material Pass

The five learned base families retain their locked functional identities and silhouettes, but now express their named Curio House materials directly in the owned Godot renderer:

- Moon Sigil → smoked moonstone.
- Thorn Rose → carved wine enamel.
- Luna Moth → spectral glass.
- Curio Key → antique metal.
- Night Ivy → lacquered leaf.

The persistent Moon Cameo in the House shares the Moon Sigil's smoked-moonstone material grammar so Level 1 gameplay and its world consequence read as one product system.

No paid or external visual asset is used for this material pass.
